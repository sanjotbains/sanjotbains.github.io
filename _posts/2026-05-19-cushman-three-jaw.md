The three jaw is the most versatile chuck for the lathe, it practically lives in the spindles of most.

![Rusty Three-Jaw Installed]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/installed_3jaw_rusty.jpg)

The chuck goes into the Evaporust a crow and return a milk white dove.

![Front After Evaporust]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/evaporust_front.jpg)
![Back After Evaporust]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/evaporust_back.jpg)

The jaws and hardware similarly are made anew.

![Jaws and Hardware Removed]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/hardware_removed.jpg)

With some percussive persuasion, the two halves come apart enough to get a prying tool between them.

![Split Halves]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/split_halves.jpg)

The gears and the scroll face are well covered in old chips and grease. 
It seems that someone took the zerk fitting on the front as a sign to pump this mostly full of grease, but this is an inappropriate lubricant. Many of these old machine tools used zerks to deliver way oil to the internals. 

![Split Open]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/split_open.jpg)

![Jaw Scroll Closeup]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/jaw_scroll_closeup.jpg)

Thankfully, because of the rather slow and relatively unoften actuation, the wear is mostly on unimportant surfaces.

Scrubbing the old grease out of the gears and internal cavities was an arduous task, but straightforward.

All cleaned, we can begin reassembly. 

![Locating Pin]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/locating_pin.jpg)

![Rear Half Assembly]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/rear_half_assembly.jpg)

![Reassembly]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/reassembly.jpg)

![Installed Jaws]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/installed_jaws1.jpg)

![Installed Jaws]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/sheldon/three_jaw/installed_jaws2.jpg)
