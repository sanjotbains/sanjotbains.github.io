A friend of mine asked me yesterday to reproduce a blockoff plate for his BMW motorcycle. He is all about weight reduction; every single gram possible must be shed.
In the pursuit of this end, he wants to replace this aluminum part with a plastic one. He tells me that these retail for about $35 online, and I was fairly confident that I could make it for less. 

As with almost every part I design or copy, I start with a set of calipers and pencil and paper.

![Scratchpad]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/blockoff/scratchpad.jpg)

What I really enjoyed about this one was how the four dimensions, plus a reasonable assumption of tangency, fully constrain the footprint of the flange.

![Flange Sketch]({{ site.baseurl }}/assets/img/blockoff/flange_sketch.png)
